1. **Lesharii** - The Lesharii are nomadic hunters of the wilderness. Their skin is covered with tribal patterns, and their eyes shine with a bestial gleam. They excel in the art of hunting, using bows and summoning creatures of nature to track their prey.
	- **Feral Hunter**: Mastering the art of the hunt, the Feral Hunter is agile and deadly, excelling in the use of ranged weapons and projectile handling. In addition to their fierce precision, they can summon and enhance wild beasts to track their enemies with formidable efficiency.

	- **Venom Hunter**: Specialist in poisons and potions, the Venom Alchemist uses toxic substances to weaken their enemies and inflict progressive damage. Master of sneak attacks, they manipulate poisons with deadly skill, making each strike as venomous as it is formidable.
	  
	
	- **Primal Hunter**: Inspired by beast instincts, the Primal Hunter is fast and agile, capable of pouncing on enemies with deadly grace. Expert in wielding two-handed weapons such as claws and short swords, they excel in attack speed and raw power, tearing their opponents apart with animal ferocity.
    
2. **Astralians** - The Astralians are ethereal beings with cosmic reflections. Their silhouettes seem to fluctuate in time and space. Masters of temporal arcana, they manipulate cosmic energies to predict their enemies' movements and alter reality itself.
	- **Cosmic Mage**: Manipulating cosmic energies, the Cosmic Mage unleashes elemental powers and devastating spells, plunging their enemies into chaos.
	
	- **Abyssal Necromancer**: Master of the undead, the Abyssal Necromancer summons creatures of darkness to fight alongside them, spreading terror among their enemies.
	 
	- **Occultist of the Abyss**: Using dark magic and curses, the Occultist of the Abyss corrupts their enemies and condemns them to slow and painful agony.
    
3. **Torin** - The Torin are giants of stone and muscle, their robust bodies covered with ancestral runes. Their strength is legendary, and their determination unwavering. Imposing on the battlefield, they stand as living ramparts, ready to crush anything that dares to stand in their way.
	- **Ether Berserker**: Imbued with Ether and raw power, the Ether Berserker throws themselves into battle, transforming their wounds into raw Ether to crush their enemies under devastating blows.
	
	- **Blood Warrior**: The Blood Warrior uses their blood to materialize blades and other attacks to cut through their enemies, spreading terror on the battlefield.
	
	- **Iron Wall**: A living shield, the Iron Wall possesses impenetrable defense thanks to their stone skin, absorbing enemy blows and returning pain with redoubled force.
    
4. **Umbrathi** - The Umbrathi are dark and stealthy beings, moving like shadows in the night. Their forms are indistinct, seeming to dissolve into the surrounding darkness. Experts in infiltration and assassination, they are the nightmares of Vor'mir's enemies, slipping into the darkest corners to eliminate their targets.
	- **Frost Shadow**: Mastering deadly cold, the Frost Shadow numbs their enemies with ice and void attacks, freezing their souls in eternal darkness.
	
	- **Abyssal Kraken**: Embracing the power of the depths, the Abyssal Kraken summons shadow tentacles to embrace and crush their enemies, pulling them into the black abyss.
	
	- **Bounty Hunter**: An implacable tracker, the Bounty Hunter hunts their targets with deadly precision, using cunning and stealth to eliminate their prey without mercy.
	    
5. **Voidborn** - The Voidborn are prophets of the abyss, venerating the cosmic void as a primordial force. They have an insectoid appearance, and their eyes shine with hypnotic intensity. Endowed with divine powers, they can bless their allies with the favor of darkness or curse their enemies with certain death.
	- **Parasite Matriarch**: Master of genetic manipulation, the Parasite Matriarch controls living parasites to corrupt and weaken their enemies, making them vulnerable to their attacks.
	
	- **Cursed Mantle**: Enveloped in the shadow of evil, the Cursed Mantle curses their enemies with devastating curses, condemning them to slow and painful death.
	
	- **Cosmic Inquisitor**: Holder of cosmic truth, the Cosmic Inquisitor reveals the secrets of the universe to expose their enemies' weaknesses, exposing them to the implacable justice of the stars.
    
6. **Fortunians** - The Fortunians are intrepid adventurers, always in search of their next fortune. Their faces are marked by scars from past battles, and their eyes sparkle with unwavering determination. Skilled in the art of luck and cunning, they navigate through life's challenges with boldness and ingenuity.

	- **Master of Cards**: Playing with fate, the Master of Cards manipulates luck to inflict unpredictable damage on their enemies, making them fall under the weight of capricious fortune.
	
	- **Techno-Magic Engineer**: A mechanical genius, the Techno-Magic Engineer builds futuristic gadgets to control the battlefield, transforming technology into a formidable weapon.
	
	- **Artisan Tinkerer**: The Artisan Tinkerers are master craftsmen and engineers, experts in enhancing and customizing equipment and weapons. Their specialty lies in the ability to strengthen and adapt existing objects to make them more effective on the battlefield. Thanks to their unmatched expertise, they can unlock special bonuses and unique improvements to enhance their power and adapt to all situations. The Artisan Tinkerers are bold innovators, ready to push the limits of technology and magic to optimize their arsenal and dominate the battlefield.
