# Trial Room

A challenge room with **graded objectives**. Performance determines the quality of the [[Blessings|Blessing]] reward.

---

## Flow

1. Enter room
2. **Objective is revealed** (e.g., "Clear without taking damage")
3. Complete the room
4. Reward scales based on performance:
   - **Failed objective** → Minor Blessing
   - **Partial success** → Standard Blessing
   - **Full success** → Major Blessing
   - **Locked Purity** → Access to Sacred Blessings

---

## Possible Objectives

| Objective | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Survive the Wave** | Survive a wave of monsters for a set duration |
| **Speed Clear** | Clear the room before the timer ends |
| **Protect the Totem** | Defend a fragile totem from waves of enemies |
| **No Damage** | Clear the room without taking any damage |
| **Limited Skills** | Complete with restricted abilities (e.g., no spells, basic attacks only) |
| **Puzzle Challenge** | Solve a puzzle while enemies spawn intermittently |
| **Vorathros Soul** | A soul mirrors your HP—damage to you damages it. If the soul dies, trial is lost. The soul doesn't benefit from regen or leech |

---

## Rewards: Blessings

**Blessings** are pure buffs with no downside. Themed around light, order, and purity.

| Tier | Requirement | Power Level |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| **Minor** | Failed objective | Small buff |
| **Standard** | Partial success | Moderate buff |
| **Major** | Full success | Strong buff |
| **Sacred** | Locked Purity only | Exclusive, very powerful |

---

## Purity/Corruption Balance

Completing a Trial Room shifts the gauge toward **Purity**.

At **100% Purity (Locked)**:
- Only Trial Rooms will spawn (no more Corrupted Rooms)
- Access to **Sacred Blessings** (exclusive powerful rewards)

---

## Design Notes

- The graded system minimizes frustration—you always get *something*
- Objectives should be challenging but fair
- Sacred Blessings reward commitment to the Purity path
