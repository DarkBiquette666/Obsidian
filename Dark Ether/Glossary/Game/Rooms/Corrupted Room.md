# Corrupted Room

A risk/reward room where you **commit to a Corruption before fighting**. Survive to keep the power—die and lose everything.

---

## Flow

1. Enter room
2. See the **Corrupted Altar** with available [[Corruptions]]
3. **Choose your Corruption first** (commit to the deal)
4. Corrupted enemies spawn
5. **Survive** to keep the Corruption
6. Die = lose the Corruption (and the run progress)

---

## Rewards: Corruptions

**Corruptions** are powerful bonuses paired with meaningful drawbacks. Themed around darkness, chaos, and corruption.

| Tier | Requirement | Power Level |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| **Standard** | Any Corrupted Room | Bonus + Malus |
| **Abyssal** | Locked Corruption only | Extreme bonus + Extreme malus |

---

## Corruption Categories

### Resource Reduction
| Corruption | Effect |
|------------|--------|
| **Resource Drain** | Lose max mana or max health after clearing |
| **Sickness** | Lose max health after clearing |
| **Inefficient Items** | Potions and healing items are half as effective |
| **Class Lock** | One of your classes is disabled |

### Random Effects
| Corruption | Effect |
|------------|--------|
| **To the Bone** | Randomly reduced movement/attack/cast speed when entering rooms |
| **Temporal Flux** | Enemies have reduced Action Speed, but sometimes it's multiplied |

### Environmental Hazards
| Corruption | Effect |
|------------|--------|
| **Dangerous Terrain** | Traps, poison zones, or environmental damage spawn in each room |
| **Limited Vision** | Decreased light radius, fog obscures enemies |

### Stronger Enemies
| Corruption | Effect |
|------------|--------|
| **Enhanced Foes** | Enemies have random boosted stats |
| **Resurrection** | Defeated enemies revive after a delay if living enemies remain |
| **Damage Reflection** | Enemies reflect a portion of damage dealt |

### Sacrifices and Choices
| Corruption | Effect |
|------------|--------|
| **Mandatory Sacrifice** | Suffer a permanent penalty to progress (less XP) |

### Physical and Mental Effects
| Corruption | Effect |
|------------|--------|
| **Temporary Mutation** | Gain global damage but lose global defense |
| **Hallucinations** | Illusory enemies appear, others become temporarily invisible |

### Time Pressure
| Corruption | Effect |
|------------|--------|
| **Countdown Timer** | Clear the floor within a time limit or die. Kills extend the timer |
| **Slow Actions** | Rooms randomly speed up or slow down enemies |

### Boss-related
| Corruption | Effect |
|------------|--------|
| **Echoes of the Past** | Previously defeated bosses can randomly spawn and attack |
| **Over Fed** | The next boss has twice as much max health |

### Special
| Corruption | Effect |
|------------|--------|
| **Anti-Matter Orb** | A strange ball follows you, randomly charging and dealing damage. Dodging grants a buff |

---

## Purity/Corruption Balance

Completing a Corrupted Room shifts the gauge toward **Corruption**.

At **100% Corruption (Locked)**:
- Only Corrupted Rooms will spawn (no more Trial Rooms)
- Access to **Abyssal Corruptions** (exclusive extreme rewards)

---

## Design Notes

- The "commit before fighting" mechanic creates tension
- Corruptions should feel like Faustian bargains—powerful but costly
- Abyssal Corruptions reward commitment to the Corruption path
