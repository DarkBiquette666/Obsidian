# Merchant Room

A safe haven where Vorathros can purchase [[Mutations]] and supplies to adapt to the current timeline's [[Anomalies]].

---

## Flow

1. Enter room (no enemies)
2. Browse the Merchant's wares
3. Spend gold on Mutations, consumables, or healing
4. Exit when ready

---

## Available Purchases

| Category | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| **Mutations** | DNA adaptations to counter current Anomalies (6 slots max) |
| **Consumables** | Potions, temporary buffs |
| **Healing** | Restore HP/resources |
| **Rare Items** | Occasionally available special equipment |

---

## Mutations

The core purchase of the Merchant Room. Vorathros manipulates his own DNA to adapt.

| Property | Detail |
|----------|--------|
| **Slots** | 6 maximum (mirrors the 6 classes) |
| **Weight** | Tactical, not build-defining |
| **Purpose** | Patch weaknesses against current Anomalies |
| **Swapping** | Can replace existing Mutations |

**Examples:**
- "Convert 30% Physical damage to Fire"
- "Your Bloodblades inflict Bleed even on immune enemies"
- "+20% damage against the dominant enemy type"
- "Lightning Resistance +30%"

---

## Strategic Considerations

- **Gold management**: Mutations vs healing vs consumables
- **Anomaly response**: Buy Mutations that counter the current floor's Anomaly
- **Slot allocation**:
  - Spread across 6 classes to cover weaknesses
  - Or stack on one class to hyper-specialize

---

## Spawn Location

- Guaranteed **before each Floor Boss**
- Provides a preparation opportunity before major encounters

---

## Design Notes

- Merchant Rooms are the tactical decision point of each floor
- The 6-slot limit forces meaningful choices
- Gold income from Normal Rooms feeds into Merchant purchases
