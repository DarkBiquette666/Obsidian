# Normal Room

The most common room type. Standard combat encounters that form the backbone of each floor.

---

## Flow

1. Enter room
2. Enemies spawn
3. Clear all enemies
4. Receive rewards

---

## Rewards

| Reward Type | Description |
|-------------|-------------|
| **Resources** | Materials for crafting and meta-progression |
| **Consumables** | Potions and temporary items |
| **Minor Stat-ups** | Small permanent stat increases |

---

## Design Notes

- Normal Rooms are the "bread and butter" of the run
- They provide steady resource income for Merchant Room purchases
- Minor stat-ups replace the traditional roguelite "small upgrades per room"
- No special mechanics—pure combat
