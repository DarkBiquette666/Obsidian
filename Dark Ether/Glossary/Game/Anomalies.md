# Anomalies

Temporal distortions that modify the rules of each floor. Anomalies are the core roguelite variance mechanic—they challenge your build without hard-countering it.

---

## Narrative Context

Each run represents a different **timeline**. Anomalies are the unique distortions of that particular timeline—reality bending in ways that force Vorathros to adapt.

---

## Design Rules

1. **Challenge, don't hard-counter**: An Anomaly should make certain builds struggle, but never make them useless
2. **Encourage class switching**: Anomalies should make different classes shine at different times
3. **Counterable by Mutations**: Players can purchase [[Mutations]] to adapt

---

## Anomaly Categories

### Resistance Anomalies
| Anomaly | Effect |
|---------|--------|
| **Physical Fortification** | Physical Resistance +50% on all enemies |
| **Elemental Ward** | Elemental Resistance +30% on all enemies |
| **Bleed Immunity** | Enemies cannot be affected by Bleed |

### Damage Anomalies
| Anomaly | Effect |
|---------|--------|
| **Volatile Death** | Enemies explode on death (Fire damage) |
| **Lightning Surge** | Lightning damage +100%, Movement speed -20% |
| **Frost Dominion** | Elite enemies have Frost Aura |

### Healing Anomalies
| Anomaly | Effect |
|---------|--------|
| **Vampiric Realm** | No natural healing, but Lifesteal +30% |
| **Regenerating Hordes** | Enemies regenerate HP, but have -30% max HP |

### Environmental Anomalies
| Anomaly | Effect |
|---------|--------|
| **Temporal Flux** | Action Speed randomly fluctuates |
| **Shrouded Path** | Reduced visibility, enemies appear from fog |

---

## When Anomalies Are Revealed

- **Start of each floor**: The Anomaly is revealed before entering any rooms
- Players can see what they're up against and plan accordingly
- [[Merchant Room]] appears before the floor boss to allow adaptation

---

## Anomaly Stacking

As the run progresses:
- New Anomalies may **replace** previous ones
- Or they may **stack** with previous ones
- Late-game floors may have multiple active Anomalies

---

## Countering Anomalies

### Class Switching
Your 6 classes are your primary tool. Physical resistance? Switch to your Cosmic Mage.

### Mutations
Purchase [[Mutations]] at the [[Merchant Room]] to patch specific weaknesses:
- "Convert 30% Physical damage to Fire"
- "Lightning Resistance +30%"
- "Your attacks ignore 20% of enemy resistances"
