The player starts in the Lobby, where they can begin a new run at any time. When a new run is initiated, a new timeline of reality is created, and a countdown timer starts (duration to be determined).

The goal is to reach and defeat the final boss before the timer runs out. If the player dies or fails to reach the end in time, they are sent back to the Lobby with all the resources collected during the run. However, the created timeline is permanently lost and doomed.

The player must keep trying until they succeed.

Plot Twist:  
Once in front of the final boss, the screen fades to black, resembling an eye closing, and a deep, monstrous voice speaks: _"I've been watching you this whole time, Vorathros. Now it is time..."_. At that moment, the player realizes that everything they have seen until now was through the eye of The Supreme Void. For this fight, the player controls The Supreme Void and must kill Vorathros—the very character they built to be powerful throughout the game. Vorathros is now controlled by an AI that will try to defeat The Supreme Void. However, to win the game, the player (as The Supreme Void) must ultimately be killed by Vorathros.

Check The Supreme Void for details.
