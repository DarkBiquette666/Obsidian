# Rooms

Rooms are the main battlefield of Dark Ether. Each room presents a combat encounter and offers rewards upon completion.

---

## Room Types

| Room | Description | Reward |
|------|-------------|--------|
| [[Normal Room]] | Standard combat encounter | Resources, consumables, minor stat-ups |
| [[Trial Room]] | Challenge with graded objectives | [[Blessings]] (scaled to performance) |
| [[Corrupted Room]] | Choose corruption first, then survive | [[Corruptions]] (bonus + malus) |
| [[Boss Room]] | Floor boss encounter | Meta-loot, story progression |
| [[Merchant Room]] | Shop | [[Mutations]], consumables, healing |
| [[Chamber of Lost Time]] | Rare, unique mechanics | Random special reward |

---

## Purity/Corruption Balance

Trial Rooms and Corrupted Rooms are linked through the **Purity/Corruption Balance System**:

- Completing a **Trial Room** shifts the gauge toward **Purity**
- Completing a **Corrupted Room** shifts the gauge toward **Corruption**
- At **100%** on either side, you become **Locked** and gain access to exclusive rewards

See [[GDD#5.4 Purity/Corruption Balance System]] for details.
