# Perks

Major, build-defining modifications to your skills. Perks are **permanent** choices that define HOW your skills fundamentally work.

---

## Narrative Context

Perks represent Vorathros's **mastery** over a skill. Through experience and meta-progression, he unlocks deeper understanding of each ability, permanently altering how it functions.

Unlike [[Mutations]] (temporary DNA adaptations), Perks are permanent expressions of skill mastery.

---

## Properties

| Property | Detail |
|----------|--------|
| **Unlock** | Permanent (once unlocked, never lost) |
| **Equip** | Modifiable (can change between runs) |
| **Impact** | Major, build-defining |
| **Acquisition** | Unlocked through meta-progression |
| **When equipped** | Before starting a run |
| **Slots per skill** | 1-2 Perks per skill (TBD) |

---

## Key Principle

**Perks are permanently unlocked, but freely modifiable.**

Once you unlock a Perk, it's yours forever—you never lose it. However, you can **change which Perks are equipped** before each run. This means:
- Your **collection** of Perks grows permanently over time
- Your **loadout** of Perks can be adjusted to fit different builds or strategies

A Blood Warrior with "Bloodblades chain to 3 enemies" plays fundamentally differently from one with "Bloodblades explode on hit". Unlock both, and you can switch between them depending on your build.

## Perk Examples

### Blood Skills
| Skill             | Perk                 | Effect                                    |
| ----------------- | -------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| **Blood Blade**   | Chain Blades         | Adds 2 blades                             |

| **Blood Barrier** | Regenerative Barrier | Barrier slowly regenerates over time      |

## Perks vs Mutations vs Supports

| Aspect | Perks | Mutations | Supports |
|--------|-------|-----------|----------|
| **Duration** | Permanent | Per-run | Permanent |
| **Impact** | Build-defining | Tactical | Skill modifier |
| **Changes** | HOW skill works | Counters Anomalies | Adds effects |
| **Acquisition** | Meta-progression | Buy at Merchant | Drop/Craft |
| **When equipped** | Before run | During run | Before run |
| **Narrative** | Skill mastery | DNA adaptation | Rune enhancement |

---

## Unlocking Perks

Perks are unlocked through **meta-progression**:
- Defeating bosses
- Reaching Global Level milestones
- Completing specific achievements
- Finding Perk Runes in [[Chamber of Lost Time]]

---

## Design Notes

- Perks should **transform** how a skill plays, not just buff numbers
- Each skill should have multiple Perks offering different playstyles
- Perks encourage theorycrafting and build diversity
- Some Perks may have synergies with specific classes or other skills
