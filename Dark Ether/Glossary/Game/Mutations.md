# Mutations

Temporary DNA adaptations that Vorathros applies to counter the current timeline's [[Anomalies]]. Purchased at the [[Merchant Room]].

---

## Narrative Context

Vorathros is a **shapeshifter** who manipulates his own DNA—this is how he can transform into any of the 6 races. Mutations represent temporary genetic modifications he applies to adapt to specific challenges within a timeline.

Unlike [[Perks]] (which are permanent mastery), Mutations are tactical adjustments that last only for the current run.

---

## Properties

| Property | Detail |
|----------|--------|
| **Slots** | 6 maximum (mirrors the 6 classes) |
| **Duration** | Current run only |
| **Weight** | Tactical, not build-defining |
| **Purpose** | Patch weaknesses against current [[Anomalies]] |
| **Acquisition** | Purchased with gold at [[Merchant Room]] |
| **Swapping** | Can replace existing Mutations at any Merchant Room |

---

## Strategic Depth

### Slot Allocation
- **Spread**: 1 Mutation per class to cover each weakness
- **Stack**: All 6 Mutations on one class to hyper-specialize

### Resource Management
Gold must be balanced between:
- Mutations
- Consumables
- Healing

---

## Mutation Categories

### Damage Conversion
| Mutation | Effect |
|----------|--------|
| **Pyrogenic Adaptation** | Convert 30% Physical damage to Fire |
| **Cryogenic Shift** | Convert 30% Physical damage to Cold |
| **Voltaic Conversion** | Convert 30% Physical damage to Lightning |

### Resistance
| Mutation | Effect |
|----------|--------|
| **Hardened Skin** | Physical Resistance +30% |
| **Flame Retardant** | Fire Resistance +30% |
| **Thermal Regulation** | Cold Resistance +30% |
| **Grounded Cells** | Lightning Resistance +30% |

### Penetration
| Mutation | Effect |
|----------|--------|
| **Armor Piercing** | Your attacks ignore 20% of enemy Physical Resistance |
| **Elemental Piercing** | Your attacks ignore 20% of enemy Elemental Resistance |

### Status Effects
| Mutation | Effect |
|----------|--------|
| **Hemorrhagic Glands** | Your Bloodblades inflict Bleed even on immune enemies |
| **Venomous Secretion** | Your attacks have 15% chance to Poison |
| **Chilling Touch** | Your attacks have 10% chance to Chill |

### Combat
| Mutation | Effect |
|----------|--------|
| **Predator Instinct** | +20% damage against the dominant enemy type |
| **Regenerative Tissue** | Gain 5% of damage dealt as Energy Shield |
| **Threatening Presence** | Your summons taunt enemies for 2 seconds on spawn |

### Survival
| Mutation | Effect |
|----------|--------|
| **Efficient Metabolism** | Consumables are 30% more effective |
| **Rapid Coagulation** | Bleed effects on you deal 50% less damage |
| **Adaptive Immunity** | Reduce duration of negative effects by 25% |

---

## Countering Anomalies

| Anomaly | Recommended Mutations |
|---------|----------------------|
| Physical Resistance +50% | Damage Conversion, Armor Piercing |
| Enemies explode (Fire) | Fire Resistance, Hardened Skin |
| No natural healing | Regenerative Tissue, Efficient Metabolism |
| Bleed Immunity | Hemorrhagic Glands, alternate damage types |

---

## Mutations vs Perks vs Supports

| Aspect | Mutations | Perks | Supports |
|--------|-----------|-------|----------|
| **Duration** | Per-run | Permanent | Permanent |
| **Impact** | Tactical | Build-defining | Skill modifier |
| **Acquisition** | Buy at Merchant | Meta-progression | Drop/Craft |
| **When equipped** | During run | Before run | Before run |
| **Narrative** | DNA adaptation | Skill mastery | Rune enhancement |

---

## Design Notes

- Mutations should feel like "tools" not "upgrades"
- They solve specific problems, not make you generically stronger
- The 6-slot limit forces meaningful choices
- Should synergize with class switching (different Mutations for different classes)
