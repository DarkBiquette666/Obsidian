# Cognitive Decay

## Description
Anemic Curse clouds the minds of affected enemies, causing cognitive deterioration that slows their attack speed and reduces their tactical awareness.

## Mechanics
- **Attack Speed Reduction**: 30% reduction in attack frequency
- **Reaction Time**: 25% slower enemy reactions to player actions
- **Ability Cooldowns**: Enemy abilities take 20% longer to recharge
- **Stacking**: Effect intensity increases with curse strength
- **Mental Resistance**: Some enemies may have partial immunity

## Synergies
- Enhanced by curse potency and duration
- Pairs with abilities that exploit enemy positioning
- Amplified by mental manipulation effects

## Strategic Use
- Defensive tool against fast attackers
- Creates windows for complex maneuvers
- Reduces pressure in overwhelming encounters
