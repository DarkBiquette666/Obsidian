# Hemorrhagic Wounds

## Description
Anemic Curse accelerates all bleeding effects on cursed targets, causing wounds to hemorrhage more rapidly and deal increased damage.

## Mechanics
- **Acceleration Factor**: 200% bleeding tick rate
- **Damage Multiplier**: 1.5x bleeding damage
- **Duration**: Bleeding effects last 75% normal duration
- **Stacking**: Works with multiple bleeding sources
- **Synergy**: Enhanced by blood mastery

## Synergies
- Pairs with any bleeding-inflicting abilities
- Enhanced by Vital Link shared damage
- Amplified by blood power scaling

## Strategic Use
- Burst damage against cursed targets
- Quick elimination of weakened enemies
- Synergizes with hit-and-run tactics
