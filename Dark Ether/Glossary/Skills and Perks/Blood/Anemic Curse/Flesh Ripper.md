# Flesh Ripper

## Description
Anemic Curse weakens the physical integrity of cursed enemies, making their flesh more susceptible to physical damage and causing wounds to tear more easily.

## Mechanics
- **Physical Vulnerability**: +50% physical damage taken
- **Critical Hit Chance**: +15% critical chance against cursed targets
- **Wound Amplification**: Physical hits create deeper wounds
- **Damage Type**: Affects all physical damage sources
- **Stacking**: Effect stacks with other vulnerability sources

## Synergies
- Powerful with physical damage builds
- Enhanced by critical hit modifiers
- Pairs excellently with bleeding effects

## Strategic Use
- Damage amplification for physical builds
- Makes cursed targets priority elimination targets
- Synergizes with high critical hit strategies
