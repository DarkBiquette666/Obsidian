# Vitality Siphon

## Description
Anemic Curse drains the life force from cursed enemies, stealing their natural regeneration and transferring it to the player.

## Mechanics
- **Regeneration Theft**: Steals 100% of enemy regeneration
- **Player Bonus**: Adds stolen regeneration to player health regen
- **Multiple Targets**: Effect stacks from multiple cursed enemies
- **Duration**: Persists while curse remains active
- **Decay**: Stolen regeneration decays 10% per second after curse ends

## Synergies
- Enhanced by curse duration and spread
- Stacks with other regeneration sources
- Amplified by blood mastery effects

## Strategic Use
- Sustain tool for extended combat
- Counters high-regeneration enemies
- Rewards maintaining curses on multiple targets
