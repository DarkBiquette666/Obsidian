# Blood Frenzy

## Description
Anemic Curse drives affected enemies into a blood-mad frenzy, causing erratic behavior that makes them attack randomly, including their own allies.

## Mechanics
- **Frenzy Chance**: 20% chance per second to enter frenzy
- **Frenzy Duration**: 3-5 seconds of erratic behavior
- **Target Confusion**: 50% chance to attack nearest target (any faction)
- **Damage Bonus**: +25% damage while frenzied but -30% accuracy
- **Spread Mechanic**: Frenzy can spread to nearby enemies on kill

## Implementation Notes
This perk requires complex AI behavior modification and faction system integration that is not yet implemented in the current game systems.

## Synergies
- Would pair with curse spread mechanics
- Enhanced by curse duration and potency
- Potential synergy with chaos-based abilities

## Strategic Use
- Crowd control through enemy confusion
- Damage mitigation by redirecting attacks
- Area denial through unpredictable enemy behavior
