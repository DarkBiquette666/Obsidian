# Crippling Chains

## Description
Anemic Curse manifests as ethereal blood chains that bind cursed enemies, reducing their movement speed and occasionally rooting them in place.

## Mechanics
- **Movement Reduction**: 40% movement speed penalty
- **Root Chance**: 15% chance per second to root for 2 seconds
- **Chain Range**: Rooted enemies cannot move beyond 5 meters from root point
- **Break Condition**: Taking 200% of curse damage breaks chains
- **Visual**: Ethereal red chains connecting to ground

## Synergies
- Enhanced by curse duration and intensity
- Root duration scales with blood mastery
- Pairs well with area denial abilities

## Strategic Use
- Crowd control for dangerous enemies
- Prevents enemy retreats and repositioning
- Creates tactical positioning advantages
