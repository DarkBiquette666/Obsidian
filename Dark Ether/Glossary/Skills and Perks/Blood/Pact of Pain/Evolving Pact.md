# Evolving Pact

## Description
Transforms Pact of Pain into a toggle ability with progressive bonuses that increase the longer the pact remains active, but with escalating health costs.

## Mechanics
- **Toggle Mode**: Can be activated/deactivated at will
- **Progressive Bonus**: +10% power every 30 seconds (max 100%)
- **Escalating Cost**: Health drain increases by 20% every 30 seconds
- **Reset Condition**: Deactivating resets both bonus and cost
- **Minimum Duration**: Must stay active for at least 10 seconds

## Synergies
- Enhanced by health regeneration and maximum health
- Pairs well with health cost reduction effects
- Amplified by damage scaling abilities

## Strategic Use
- Risk/reward management system
- Long-term power scaling for extended battles
- Strategic activation timing for boss encounters
