# Carnage Pact

## Description
While Pact of Pain is active, enemy deaths create blood explosions that apply bleeding effects to nearby enemies, spreading carnage across the battlefield.

## Mechanics
- **Trigger**: Enemy death while pact is active
- **Explosion Range**: 4-meter radius
- **Bleeding Duration**: 6 seconds
- **Bleeding Damage**: 75% of enemy's maximum health over duration
- **Chain Reaction**: Explosion kills can trigger additional explosions

## Synergies
- Enhanced by bleeding damage modifiers
- Scales with pact power and duration
- Amplified by area damage bonuses

## Strategic Use
- Area clearing for dense enemy groups
- Cascading damage effects in crowds
- Reward for maintaining pact during combat
