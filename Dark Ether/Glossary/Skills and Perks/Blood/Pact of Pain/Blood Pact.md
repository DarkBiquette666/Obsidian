# Blood Pact

## Description
Extends Pact of Pain duration significantly, but each enemy kill reduces the remaining time. Creates a balance between power duration and combat engagement.

## Mechanics
- **Extended Duration**: Base duration increased by 200%
- **Kill Penalty**: Each kill reduces remaining time by 15%
- **Minimum Duration**: Cannot be reduced below 30 seconds
- **Elite Bonus**: Elite/boss kills reduce time by only 5%
- **Power Scaling**: Damage bonus increases as time decreases

## Synergies
- Rewards selective targeting and efficiency
- Enhanced by kill count bonuses
- Pairs with abilities that don't rely on kills

## Strategic Use
- Strategic target selection becomes crucial
- Rewards efficient, high-value eliminations
- Creates tension between power and duration
