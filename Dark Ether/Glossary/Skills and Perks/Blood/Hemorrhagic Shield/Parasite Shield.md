# Parasite Shield

## Description
Hemorrhagic Shield absorbs a portion of damage dealt by the player, converting it into additional shield strength and extending shield duration.

## Mechanics
- **Absorption Rate**: 25% of damage dealt
- **Shield Boost**: Absorbed damage adds to current shield
- **Duration Extension**: Each absorption extends shield by 0.5 seconds
- **Maximum Absorption**: 200% of base shield value
- **Conversion Type**: All damage types convert to shield

## Synergies
- Enhanced by damage-over-time effects
- Scales with attack speed and multi-hit abilities
- Pairs well with offensive blood skills

## Strategic Use
- Aggressive playstyle enhancement
- Sustain through combat engagement
- Rewards consistent damage output
