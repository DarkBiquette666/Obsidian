# Reactive Barrier

## Description
When Hemorrhagic Shield is broken, it explodes outward, dealing blood damage to all nearby enemies and applying bleeding effects.

## Mechanics
- **Trigger**: Shield break
- **Effect**: AoE explosion around player
- **Damage**: Based on remaining shield value
- **Range**: 3-meter radius
- **Status**: Applies bleeding to hit enemies
- **Cooldown**: None (tied to shield regeneration)

## Synergies
- Works with all shield enhancement perks
- Explosion damage scales with blood power
- Bleeding duration enhanced by blood mastery

## Strategic Use
- Defensive counter-attack mechanism
- Area denial when overwhelmed
- Punishes aggressive enemy positioning
