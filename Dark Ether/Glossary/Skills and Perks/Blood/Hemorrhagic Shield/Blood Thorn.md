# Blood Thorn

## Description
Enemies that damage Hemorrhagic Shield suffer bleeding damage and have a chance to contract a spreading blood curse.

## Mechanics
- **Trigger**: Taking damage while shield is active
- **Bleeding Damage**: 150% of absorbed damage over 5 seconds
- **Spread Chance**: 30% on enemy death with curse
- **Spread Range**: 2-meter radius
- **Stack Limit**: Up to 3 stacks of bleeding per enemy

## Synergies
- Enhanced by shield absorption and reflection effects
- Bleeding spreads through blood contagion mechanics
- Scales with shield strength and duration

## Strategic Use
- Punishes sustained enemy attacks
- Creates cascading damage effects
- Defensive tool with offensive potential
