# Toxic Explosion

## Description
Upon player death or when manually triggered at low health, creates a massive explosion that applies both bleeding and poison effects to all nearby enemies.

## Mechanics
- **Death Trigger**: Automatic activation on death
- **Manual Trigger**: Can be activated below 25% health
- **Explosion Range**: 8-meter radius
- **Bleeding Duration**: 10 seconds
- **Poison Duration**: 15 seconds
- **Damage Scaling**: Based on accumulated poison stacks
- **Survival Chance**: 15% chance to survive at 1 HP after manual trigger

## Synergies
- Enhanced by poison and bleeding modifiers
- Scales with maximum poison stacks
- Amplified by area effect bonuses

## Strategic Use
- Last resort defensive mechanism
- Area denial through status effects
- Strategic sacrifice for team benefit
