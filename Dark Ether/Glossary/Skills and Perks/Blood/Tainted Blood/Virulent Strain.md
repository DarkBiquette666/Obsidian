# Virulent Strain

## Description
The poison within Tainted Blood becomes more potent, amplifying all poison effects on the player and converting them into beneficial blood toxins.

## Mechanics
- **Poison Amplification**: All poison effects deal 150% normal damage
- **Toxin Conversion**: 25% of poison damage converts to blood power
- **Duration Extension**: Poison effects last 50% longer
- **Resistance Building**: Gain 5% poison resistance per stack (max 50%)
- **Beneficial Mutation**: High poison stacks grant temporary damage bonus

## Synergies
- Enhanced by poison application abilities
- Pairs with blood power scaling effects
- Amplified by damage conversion mechanics

## Strategic Use
- Turns poison weakness into strength
- Rewards poison accumulation strategy
- Provides both offense and defense benefits
