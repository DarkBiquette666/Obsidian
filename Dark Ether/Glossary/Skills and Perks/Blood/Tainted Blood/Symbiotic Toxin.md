# Symbiotic Toxin

## Description
Creates a symbiotic relationship with nearby poisoned enemies, where their presence reduces the player's own poison damage through shared toxin load.

## Mechanics
- **Poison Reduction**: 10% self-poison reduction per nearby poisoned enemy
- **Range**: 6-meter detection radius
- **Maximum Reduction**: 80% total reduction with 8+ enemies
- **Shared Load**: Enemy poison stacks partially count as player stacks
- **Symbiotic Healing**: Poisoned enemy deaths provide minor health restoration

## Synergies
- Enhanced by poison spreading abilities
- Pairs with crowd gathering tactics
- Amplified by poison duration extensions

## Strategic Use
- Encourages fighting in poisoned areas
- Rewards spreading poison to multiple enemies
- Provides defensive scaling in dense encounters
