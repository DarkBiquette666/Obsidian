# Blood Purge

## Description
Allows the player to voluntarily convert all accumulated poison stacks into a period of complete invulnerability, purging the blood of toxins at the cost of temporary weakness afterward.

## Mechanics
- **Conversion Rate**: Each poison stack grants 0.5 seconds of invulnerability
- **Maximum Duration**: 10 seconds of invulnerability (20 stacks)
- **Activation Requirement**: Must have at least 5 poison stacks
- **Post-Purge Weakness**: 50% damage penalty for 15 seconds after
- **Cooldown**: 120 seconds between activations

## Synergies
- Enhanced by poison accumulation strategies
- Pairs with high-risk, high-reward playstyles
- Amplified by invulnerability extension effects

## Strategic Use
- Emergency defensive tool
- Tactical timing for dangerous encounters
- Risk management for poison-heavy builds
