# Ephemeral Barrier

## Description
The Blood Barrier becomes a brief moment of perfect invulnerability, granting complete immunity to all damage for a short duration instead of providing charge-based protection. This creates a high-skill, timing-based defensive option.

## Mechanics

### Invulnerability System
- **Duration:** 2-3 seconds of complete immunity
- **Protection:** 100% damage immunity
- **No Charges:** Cannot be depleted early
- **All Damage Types:** Physical, magical, DoT immunity
- **Status Protection:** Immune to debuffs during duration

### Timing Mechanics
- **Fixed Duration:** Cannot be extended or reduced
- **Instant Activation:** Immediate invulnerability on cast
- **Precise Window:** Exact timing critical for effectiveness
- **Cooldown Start:** Begins when invulnerability ends
- **Animation Lock:** Brief cast time vulnerability

### Perfect Defense
- **Damage Immunity:** All incoming damage negated
- **Status Immunity:** All debuffs ignored
- **DoT Suppression:** Existing DoTs paused
- **Environmental:** Immune to environmental damage
- **Execution Protection:** Cannot be executed during effect
