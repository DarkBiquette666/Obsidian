# Regenerative Barrier

## Description
The Blood Barrier pulses with regenerative energy, providing continuous healing to the caster while active. This transforms the barrier from pure defense into a healing sanctuary that combines protection with recovery.

## Mechanics

### Healing System
- **Healing Rate:** 3% max health per second
- **Duration:** Full barrier duration
- **Stacking:** Does not stack with multiple barriers
- **Scaling:** Scales with healing modifiers
- **Type:** Health regeneration (not instant healing)

### Regeneration Calculation
- **Base Rate:** 3% maximum health per second
- **Modifier Support:** Affected by healing bonuses
- **Duration Scaling:** Total healing = rate × duration
- **Efficiency:** Consistent healing throughout duration
- **Cap:** No maximum healing limit

### Integration Mechanics
- **Barrier Dependency:** Healing stops when barrier ends
- **Damage Interruption:** Continues healing even while taking damage
- **Full Health:** Continues at full health (no overheal)
- **Status Immunity:** Not affected by anti-heal debuffs
- **Source Type:** Blood magic healing
