# Persistent Barrier

## Description
The Blood Barrier becomes more persistent but less dense, extending its duration significantly while reducing the protection it provides per charge. This creates a sustained defensive option for prolonged engagements.

## Mechanics

### Duration Extension
- **Base Modifier:** +100% duration
- **Protection Reduction:** -40% damage absorbed per charge
- **Total Charges:** Unchanged
- **Efficiency:** More time, less absorption per hit
- **Coverage:** Extended defensive window

### Protection Calculation
- **Base Absorption:** Standard charge protection
- **Reduction Factor:** 60% of original absorption
- **Duration Increase:** Double base duration
- **Charge Efficiency:** Lower per-hit protection
- **Total Protection:** Potentially higher over time

### Balance Mechanics
- **Time vs Intensity:** Trade burst protection for duration
- **Sustained Value:** Better against consistent damage
- **Burst Vulnerability:** Weaker against spike damage
- **Resource Allocation:** Spread protection over time
- **Strategic Timing:** Longer defensive windows
