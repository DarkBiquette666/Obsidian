# Barrier Symbiosis

## Description
Blood Blade forms a symbiotic bond with active Blood Barriers, creating additional phantom blades that manifest alongside each protective barrier. This network of interconnected blood magic creates incredible offensive potential, but with the ultimate risk - if any barrier falls, all blades vanish simultaneously, making defense and offense inseparable.

## Lore
The pinnacle of blood magic integration, where offense and defense merge into a singular living network. Each barrier becomes a nexus point for blade manifestation, creating a web of crimson protection and aggression. Yet this unity comes with vulnerability - break one link, and the entire network shatters like glass.

## Mechanics

### Symbiosis System
- **Blade Generation:** +1 Blood Blade per active Blood Barrier
- **Maximum Blades:** 5 total (base 1 + 4 barriers maximum)
- **Shared Properties:** All blades inherit barrier-granted bonuses
- **Unified Destruction:** ANY barrier breaking destroys ALL blades
- **Formation Requirement:** Must have at least 1 active Blood Barrier

### Blade Network Properties
| Barriers Active | Total Blades | Blade Efficiency | Network Risk |
|-----------------|--------------|------------------|--------------|
| **0** | 0 | No symbiosis | No risk |
| **1** | 2 | 90% each | Low risk |
| **2** | 3 | 85% each | Medium risk |  
| **3** | 4 | 80% each | High risk |
| **4** | 5 | 75% each | Maximum risk |

### Barrier Property Inheritance
- **Parasitic Barrier:** All blades heal on hit (2% max HP)
- **Regenerative Barrier:** All blades have reduced health costs (-25%)
- **Persistent Barrier:** All blades gain extended duration (+50%)
- **Ephemeral Barrier:** All blades gain increased attack speed (+30%)
