# Corrosive Link

## Description
Applies armor reduction to the linked enemy, making them more vulnerable to physical damage. The corrosive effect weakens the target's defenses while the vital link drains their life force.

## Mechanics

### Effect Details
- **Primary Effect:** Applies armor reduction debuff to linked enemy
- **Damage Amplification:** Increases Physical damage taken by target
- **Duration:** Active while link is maintained
- **Stacking:** May stack with other armor reduction sources
- **Visual Effect:** Greenish corrosive particles along the blood chain

### Tactical Value
- Makes heavily armored enemies vulnerable
- Amplifies physical damage from all sources
- Synergizes perfectly with physical damage builds
- Enables faster elimination of tough targets

## Synergies

### Primary Synergies
- **Blood Blade:** Direct damage amplification for blade attacks
- **Physical Builds:** All physical damage sources benefit
- **Team Play:** Allies also benefit from armor reduction

### Build Integration
- Essential for physical damage focused Torin builds
- Transforms Vital Link from pure sustain to damage support
- Creates aggressive positioning opportunities

## Strategic Usage

### Target Priority
- High-armor enemies become primary targets
- Bosses and elites particularly vulnerable
- Tanks lose their primary defense

### Positioning Considerations
- Requires maintaining link for debuff
- Risk vs reward for staying close
- Balance between safety and effectiveness
