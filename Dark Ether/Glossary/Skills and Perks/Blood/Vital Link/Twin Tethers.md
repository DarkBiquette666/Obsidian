# Twin Tethers

## Description
Enables the maintenance of two Vital Links simultaneously at double the mana cost. Life drain is divided between both targets, exponentially increasing tactical complexity while offering unprecedented sustain coverage.

## Mechanics

### Dual Link System
- **Maximum Links:** 2 simultaneous connections
- **Independent Tracking:** Each link separate
- **Activation Cost:** Double mana per cast
- **Maintenance:** No additional ongoing cost
- **Target Selection:** Choose two enemies

### Drain Distribution
- **Split Efficiency:** 50% drain per target
- **Total Drain:** 100% combined (base rate)
- **Independent Calculation:** Each link separate
- **Range Management:** Individual distances
- **Break Conditions:** Links break independently

### Resource Requirements
- **Initial Cost:** 2x base mana cost
- **Activation:** Can link simultaneously or sequentially
- **Reactivation:** Full cost for replacement
- **Efficiency Trade:** More coverage, same total drain
