# Sanguine Refuge

## Description
Grants 75% damage resistance for the first 2 seconds after establishing a Vital Link. This temporary protection window allows safe engagement and link establishment in dangerous situations.

## Mechanics

### Protection Window
- **Damage Reduction:** 75% all damage types
- **Duration:** First 2 seconds only
- **Activation:** Instant on link creation
- **One-Time Use:** Per link establishment
- **Reactivation:** New link = new protection

### Timing Mechanics
- **Countdown Start:** Moment of connection
- **Fixed Duration:** Always 2 seconds
- **No Extension:** Cannot be prolonged
- **Clear Indicator:** Visual timer display
- **Expiration Warning:** Audio/visual cue

### Damage Mitigation
- **All Sources:** Physical, elemental, DoT
- **Stacking:** May stack with other defenses
- **Calculation Order:** Applied after armor/resistance
- **True Reduction:** Works on all damage types
