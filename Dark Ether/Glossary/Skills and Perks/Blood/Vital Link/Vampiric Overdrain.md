# Vampiric Overdrain

## Description
Increased the life drain rate of Vital Link by 30% while the play doesn't move and the link is active. This perk transforms the caster into a stationary vampire, trading mobility for exceptional sustain.

## Mechanics

### Drain Amplification
- **Life Drain Rate:** 130% of base value
- **Healing Per Second:** Doubled effectiveness
- **Resource Efficiency:** Same mana, double healing
- **Stacking:** Multiplicative with other bonuses

### Movement Penalty
- **Duration:** Active while link maintained
- **Instant Application:** No ramp-up time
- **Recovery:** Normal speed on link break

### Visual Effects
- **Intensified Chain:** Thicker, more vibrant blood link
- **Drain Particles:** Enhanced life force visuals
- **Movement Indicator:** Speed debuff icon
- **Vampiric Aura:** Dark energy around caster

## Risk vs Reward Analysis

### Advantages
- **Superior Sustain:** Exceptional healing rate
- **Tank Potential:** Outlast enemies through drain
- **Resource Efficiency:** Maximum healing per mana
- **Boss Fighting:** Incredible for stationary targets

### Disadvantages
- **Mobility Loss:** Vulnerable to area attacks
- **Positioning Risk:** Harder to avoid mechanics
- **Escape Difficulty:** Reduced retreat options
- **Kiting Impossible:** Can't maintain distance

## Strategic Usage

### Ideal Scenarios
- **Tank Builds:** Absorb damage while healing
- **Stationary Combat:** Fight without moving
- **High Defense:** Compensate mobility with armor
- **Team Support:** Protected by allies

### Build Requirements
- **Defensive Stats:** High armor/resistance needed
- **Crowd Control:** Stuns/slows to control enemies
- **Positioning Skills:** Careful engagement planning
- **Escape Options:** Emergency link break strategies

## Synergies

### Defensive Combinations
- **Blood Barrier:** Shield while immobile
- **Hemorrhagic Shield:** Additional protection
- **Sanguine Refuge:** Initial damage reduction

### Offensive Synergies
- **Hemorrhagic Catalyst:** Stack with bleed bonus
- **Corrosive Link:** Faster kills reduce risk
- **Twin Tethers:** Double drain sources

### Tactical Synergies
- **Crowd Control:** Lock enemies in place
- **Area Denial:** Control space while draining
- **Team Coordination:** Allies provide protection
