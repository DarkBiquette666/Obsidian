# Symbiosis Mastery

## Description
The Vital Link becomes bidirectional - the enemy can heal when you take damage, but the life drain rate increases by 200%. This ultimate risk vs reward perk requires perfect damage management and represents the pinnacle of Vital Link mastery.

## Mechanics

### Bidirectional System
- **Player → Enemy:** Standard drain (200% rate)
- **Enemy → Player:** Reverse heal on damage taken
- **Simultaneous Flow:** Both directions active
- **Net Calculation:** Drain minus reverse heal
- **Real-Time Balance:** Constant flux

### Enhanced Drain Rate
- **Base Multiplier:** 200% increase (3x total)
- **Stacks Multiplicatively:** With other bonuses
- **Massive Potential:** Can reach 6x+ with full build
- **Resource Efficiency:** Triple healing per mana

### Reverse Healing Mechanics
- **Trigger:** Any damage taken by player
- **Heal Amount:** Percentage of damage taken
- **Enemy Recovery:** Restores enemy health
- **No Cap:** Enemy can heal to full
- **Instant Application:** No delay
