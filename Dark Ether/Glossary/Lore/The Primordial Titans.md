Before the coming of chaos and conflict, the Five Titans reigned as entities shaping the universe-guardians of an eternal cycle in which everything found its place. Their essence was intimately linked to the Dark Ether, the primordial substance that flows through reality, source of all creation and all endings. The Titans were not mere wardens or forces: they were the architects of the cosmos, molding worlds, life, and destiny through their unified will.

Together, they wielded the Dark Ether not as a weapon, but as the supreme tool of balance. Through their hands, life emerged, flourished, and then faded, only to be reborn in new forms. Their powers complemented each other: where one fostered growth, another orchestrated the necessary destruction for regeneration; where one guided the light, another wove the shadow to preserve harmony. They ensured that every world respected the grand cycle, preventing both excess and stagnation, correcting imbalances through acts of creation or dissolution.

Their role transcended that of mere protectors: they embodied the fundamental law of the universe-an infinite cycle where birth, transformation, and death endlessly succeed one another. United, the Titans are the keystone of cosmic stability: without them, the Dark Ether could tip toward corruption or entropy, and the very order of things would be threatened.

Thus, the Five Titans are not simply creator gods: they are the living memory of the cycle, the invisible regulators of life and death, and the guarantors of a balance meant to last for eternity.
