- Appearance: Kyrektor stands tall with a humanoid body composed of swirling storm clouds, its skin a mesmerizing dance of electric blue and white clouds. Its eyes burn with intense electric blue light, and its hair is a wild tangle of lightning-scorched stormcloud tendrils. Two massive wings made of layered stormclouds sprout from its back, crackling with electricity. Its body is adorned with glowing, ethereal circuitry patterns that pulse with storm energy.
- Abilities:

	- Storm Surge: Unleashes a wave of electricity that damages and stuns Vorathros.
	- Wind Shear: Creates a localized storm that lifts Vorathros off the ground and deals damage.
	- Eye of the Storm: Temporarily becomes invulnerable and summons a swirling storm around itself, damaging Vorathros.
