- Appearance: Pyropex is a hulking, lava-covered creature with a body resembling a twisted, molten furnace. Its skin is a constantly shifting, burning landscape of lava flows and smoldering embers. Its eyes glow like hot coals, and its massive arms end in claws that seem to be made of solidified lava. A fiery aura surrounds its body, and flames dance across its surface, casting flickering shadows on the environment.
- Abilities:

	- Lava Burst: Erupts with a wave of molten lava that damages Vorathros and sets the ground on fire.
	- Flame Shield: Surrounds itself with a wall of flames, making it resistant to attacks.
	- Inferno: Unleashes a massive blast of flames that deals massive damage to Vorathros.
