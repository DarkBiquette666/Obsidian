- Appearance: Xarenthor appears as a tall, gaunt figure shrouded in darkness, its body a void-like presence that seems to absorb light around it. Its eyes burn with an otherworldly green glow, and its long, spindly limbs end in delicate-looking fingers that seem to weave and manipulate the shadows. Dark, tendril-like appendages sprout from its back, writhing like living darkness.

- Abilities:

	- Shadow Slip: Teleports short distances through shadows, making it difficult to hit.
	- Darkness: Creates a cloud of darkness that reduces Vorathros' accuracy and increases damage taken.
	- Shadow Tentacles: Summons tendrils of darkness that grab and damage Vorathros.
