Nihalus is a massive being made of a dark, constantly shifting substance, like mist. His body is covered in thick chitin armor but deformed by the Dark Ether, giving him a terrifying yet majestic appearance. Shards of green light escape from his body, suggesting a necrotic power trapped within. His eyes glow with an intense green light, and he has elongated arms ending in sharp claws. Tentacle-like appendages protrude from his back, moving independently of his body.

## Nihalus Phase 2 (66% Health)
Transformation and Empowerment When he reaches 66% health, Nihalus absorbs the Dark Ether around him and changes form. His arms transform into crystalline blades, and his tentacles become more numerous and aggressive.

- Explosive Ether: Nihalus charges his energy and releases a wave of Dark Ether that quickly spreads across the ground. The player must take cover behind the Dark Ether crystals left by his previous attacks to avoid massive damage.
- Cooldown: 15 seconds
- Damage Ratio: 25/100 (This attack deals heavy damage and forces the player to position correctly, but the longer CD provides time to react.)


## Nihalus Phase 3 (33% Health)
Rage of the Abyss In a final effort, Nihalus fuses all his energies, transforming the arena into a Dark Ether.

- Ether Portals: Nihalus opens Dark Ether portals around the arena, from which fast-moving projectiles emerge. The player must constantly move to avoid these unpredictable attacks.
- Cooldown: 6 seconds
- Damage Ratio: 7/100 per projectile (The portals are frequent, and the projectiles are hard to dodge, but individual damage isn’t extremely high.)

- Devouring Ether: The boss attempts to pull the player toward him, slowing their movements. Meanwhile, tentacles appear on the sides of the arena, attacking unexpectedly.

- Cooldown: 15 seconds
- Damage Ratio: No direct damage but reduces the player’s movement speed by 50% for 5 seconds. Tentacles deal 10/100 damage per hit.

# Story:

  
## The Story of Nihalus - The Abyssal Warden

Before being corrupted by the Dark Ether, Nihalus was once a being of nature, a respected general who served the Titan of Life, Sol'Kanar. He was renowned for his wisdom, mastery over the forces of nature, and unwavering dedication to protecting the worlds under Sol'Kanar’s benevolent rule. However, during the great corruption brought about by the Dark Ether, Syl'Korath himself succumbed to this destructive power, and his generals were dragged down with him.

Nihalus, as the most loyal and powerful general, was directly exposed to the influence of the Dark Ether. Instead of destroying him, this malevolent force twisted his mind and body, transforming his talents for creation into a power of destruction. His control over vital energies became a dark necrotic force, enabling him to manipulate the souls of the dead and create undead servants from the black substance that envelops him.

## Nihalus’ Role in the Conflict Against the Titans

After his corruption, Nihalus became the chief general of Sol'Kanar’s necrotic armies— Syl'Korath now fully corrupted and renamed Syl'Korath the Infamous.’ As the Abyssal Guardian, Nihalus is tasked with defending access to his master and spreading terror across worlds conquered by the Dark Ether. His ability to draw upon the dark energies of the Dark Ether allows him to awaken the dead and transform them into soulless warriors loyal to the Titan’s will.

In the world of _Dark Ether_, Nihalus represents a major obstacle for Vorathros. He is one of the final barriers between the hero and Sol'Kanar, and his presence weighs heavily on the world that the shapeshifter seeks to liberate. Nihalus’ corruption runs so deep that he appears as an echo of what he once was—a creature both majestic and terrifying—whose sole mission is to expand the influence of the Dark Ether.

## The Confrontation with Vorathros

When the shapeshifter ventures into the Abyss—a domain isolated from the rest of existence and anchored in the dark energies of the Dark Ether—Nihalus emerges as its ultimate guardian. Aware of the power that Vorathros represents, Nihalus sees him as a threat to his master’s dominion. Though he retains a fraction of his memories from when he served under the light, he allows no hesitation to deter him from his role. He summons spirits of the dead, shapes creatures from Dark Ether, and uses his own altered form to crush anyone who dares challenge Sol'Kanar’s will.

## The Final Moments of Nihalus

When he is ultimately defeated by Vorathros, Nihalus regains a brief moment of clarity. He murmurs a few words about who he once was and about the pain of serving a corrupted master whom he had once loved and respected. He warns Vorathros of Sol'Kanar’s power and madness before disappearing into a whirlwind of Dark Ether, leaving behind a fragment of his power that can be used by Vorathros in preparation for facing the Titan.

## Integration into Lore

Nihalus serves as an example of corruption and downfall caused by the Dark Ether. His story underscores how destructive this power can be—even for beings who were once pure and devoted. His transformation into a necromantic general reflects themes central to _Dark Ether_: duality between life and death, light and darkness.

As a major boss, Nihalus embodies the might of Titan generals and prepares Vorathros for his final confrontation against these ancient beings. He adds another layer of depth to the story, showing that Dark Ether is far more than just an evil energy—it is a force capable of warping and destroying even the noblest souls.
