## Spectres - The Damned Souls of Nihalus

The **Spectres** are the tragic remnants of warriors who once fought valiantly under Nihalus’ command. After their decapitation at the hands of their corrupted general, their suffering did not end with death. Nihalus, wielding his mastery over necrotic energies and the Dark Ether, captured the very souls of his fallen soldiers and imprisoned them within cursed phylacteries. These vessels, infused with concentrated Dark Ether, subjected the trapped souls to endless torment—a ceaseless cycle of agony and despair that eroded their essence over time.

What emerged from this process was no longer a soul but a corrupted entity, stripped of its humanity and filled with magical hatred. These tortured spirits were reforged into **Spectres**, intangible beings composed of swirling black etheric matter. Their forms are amorphous and ever-shifting, resembling dark wisps or shadowy tendrils that drift ominously through the air. They are bound to Nihalus’ will, serving as his emissaries of destruction and terror on the battlefield.

## The Creation Process

The transformation into a Spectre is as cruel as it is deliberate. After capturing a warrior’s soul, Nihalus seals it inside a phylactery carved from obsidian-like material and drenched in Dark Ether. The soul is then subjected to an unrelenting torrent of pain—its memories, identity, and willpower slowly consumed by the corruptive force of the Ether. This process can take days or even weeks, during which the soul’s cries echo faintly from within the phylactery, a haunting reminder of its suffering.

Once fully corrupted, the soul loses all semblance of its former self and becomes an obedient wisp of dark energy. The phylactery shatters as the Spectre emerges, its new form intangible yet brimming with destructive magical power.

## Behaviour and Abilities

Spectres are relentless hunters, driven by an insatiable need to inflict pain upon others—a reflection of the torment they endured in their creation. They float silently through the air, their presence marked only by faint whispers or chilling gusts that precede their arrival. While they lack physical bodies, they are no less dangerous; their attacks consist of launching searing projectiles of Dark Ether at their enemies.

- **Dark Ether Bolts:** Spectres hurl volatile orbs of Dark Ether that explode on impact, dealing magical damage and leaving residual corruption in their wake.
    
- **Intangible Form:** When the attacker is to close, they change into intangible matter to flee further.
    
- **Aura of Dread:** Their proximity instils fear in weaker beings, sapping their courage and weakening their resolve.
