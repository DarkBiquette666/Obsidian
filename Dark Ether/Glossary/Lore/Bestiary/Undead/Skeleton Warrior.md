## **Lore**


The Skeleton Warriors were once noble soldiers, loyal to their general, Nihalus, and the Titan of Life, Sol'Kanar. They fought valiantly to defend their world under the radiant banner of life and nature. However, when the Dark Ether corrupted Sol'Kanar, it dragged Nihalus and his forces into darkness. Twisted by the overwhelming power of this malevolent force, Nihalus became a harbinger of destruction, his mind and body warped into a vessel of necrotic energy.

In his descent into madness, Nihalus committed an act of unparalleled cruelty: he personally tore the heads from every one of his soldiers with his own hands. Through this horrific ritual, he bound their souls to their decapitated bodies using the dark etheric energy that consumed him. These warriors were reborn as undead husks, stripped of their identities and free will, cursed to serve Nihalus for eternity. Now, they roam the battlefield as relentless Skeleton Warriors, dragging their rusted blades behind them as echoes of their former glory.

Though they are slow and mindless in their movements, these undead soldiers embody the tragic legacy of Nihalus—a reminder of the devastating corruption wrought by the Dark Ether.

## **Abilities**

## 1. Rusted Blade Strike

- **Description:** The Skeleton Warrior swings its heavy, rusted sword in a slow but powerful arc, aiming to cleave through anything in its path.
    
- **Damage Type:** Physical
    
- **Range:** Melee
    
- **Effect:** Deals moderate physical damage to the player. The attack is slow but has a wide hitbox, punishing careless positioning.
    

## 2. Relentless Advance _(Passive)_

- **Description:** The Skeleton Warrior moves slowly toward the player with unwavering determination, dragging its sword along the ground.
    
- **Effect:** Constantly advances toward the player at a steady pace. Cannot be staggered or knocked back easily due to its undead resilience.
    

## 3. Etheric Resilience _(Passive)_

- **Description:** As an undead creature fueled by dark ether, the Skeleton Warrior is resistant to all type of damage (10%).
    
- **Effect:** Reduced damage from dark magic attacks and immune to fear-based effects.
    

## **Behavior**

- The Skeleton Warrior is slow-moving but persistent, making it a threat in tight spaces or when paired with faster enemies.
    
- It prioritizes melee combat and will attempt to corner the player.
    
- Its attack animations are deliberate and easy to predict but deal significant damage if not avoided.
