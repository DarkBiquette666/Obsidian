The Voidborn are prophets of the abyss, venerating the cosmic void as a primordial force. They have an insectoid appearance, and their eyes shine with hypnotic intensity. Endowed with divine powers, they can bless their allies with the favor of darkness or curse their enemies with certain death.

## Class Mechanic: Infestation
Many Voidborn spells apply stacks of **Infestation**. Minions prioritize infested enemies and deal increased damage to them.
