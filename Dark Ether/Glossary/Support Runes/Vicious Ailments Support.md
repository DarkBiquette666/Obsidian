# Vicious Ailments Support
![[Assets/Vicious_Ailments_Rune.svg|128]]

## Description
A Support Rune that specializes the supported skill for Damage Over Time (DoT), significantly increasing the damage of ailments applied (Bleed, Poison, Ignite) at the cost of reduced direct hit damage.

## Mechanics

### Progression
Each level increases the damage of ailments. Level 5 unlocks an **Alternate Behavior**.

| Level | Effect | Trade-off |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Level 1** | **+30%** More Damage with Ailments | **-30%** Less Damage with Hits |
| **Level 2** | **+40%** More Damage with Ailments | **-30%** Less Damage with Hits |
| **Level 3** | **+50%** More Damage with Ailments | **-30%** Less Damage with Hits |
| **Level 4** | **+60%** More Damage with Ailments | **-30%** Less Damage with Hits |
| **Level 5 (Alt)** | **Pandemic:** Killing an enemy affected by an ailment spreads a copy of that ailment to enemies within 4m. | **-20%** Less Damage with Ailments.<br>Spread ailments cannot spread again. |

## Compatible Skills
- **Blood Blade:** Maximizes the 200% bleed factor.
- **Corrosive Bile:** Enhances poison damage.
- **Any DoT Skill:** Ideal for [[Bleed]], [[Poison]], or [[Burn]] builds.

## Strategic Value
- **Boss Killer (Lvl 1-4):** Massive single-target DoT scaling.
- **Clear Speed (Lvl 5):** The Pandemic variant solves the issue of single-target DoTs having poor clear speed.
