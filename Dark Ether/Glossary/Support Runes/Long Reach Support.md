# Long Reach Support
![[Assets/Long_Reach_Rune.svg|128]]

## Description
A Support Rune designed exclusively to extend the travel distance of projectiles, the length of beams, and the reach of melee strikes. **Does not affect Area of Effect.**

## Mechanics

### Progression
Each level increases Projectile Speed and Melee/Beam Range. Level 5 unlocks an **Alternate Behavior**.

| Level | Effect | Trade-off |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Level 1** | **+30%** Projectile Speed & Melee Range | **None** |
| **Level 2** | **+40%** Projectile Speed & Melee Range | **None** |
| **Level 3** | **+50%** Projectile Speed & Melee Range | **None** |
| **Level 4** | **+60%** Projectile Speed & Melee Range | **None** |
| **Level 5 (Alt)** | **Sniper:** **+100%** Projectile Speed & Range. | **Far Shot:** Deal up to **30% more damage** to distant targets.<br>**Point Blank:** Deal **50% less damage** to nearby targets. |

## Compatible Skills
- **Blood Blade:** Extends the orbit radius and attack reach.
- **Vital Link:** Maintains links across the screen.
- **Melee Skills:** Turns melee strikes into mid-range attacks (e.g., hitting enemies without entering their hitboxes).
- **Projectile Skills:** Increases travel distance (effective range).

## Strategic Value
- **Safety:** Allows dealing damage from outside enemy retaliation range.
- **Sniper (Lvl 5):** Rewards perfect spacing and long-range playstyles, punishing poor positioning.
