# Chain Support
![[Assets/Chain_Rune.svg|128]]

## Description
Projectiles gain the ability to chain (bounce) to new targets after hitting an enemy. This is excellent for clearing packs of enemies but comes with a damage penalty for each chain.

## Mechanics

### Progression
Each level improves the chain count or damage retention. Level 5 unlocks an **Alternate Behavior**.

| Level | Effect | Trade-off |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Level 1** | **+1** Chain Times | **-20%** Damage per Chain |
| **Level 2** | **+2** Chain Times | **-15%** Damage per Chain |
| **Level 3** | **+2** Chain Times | **-10%** Damage per Chain |
| **Level 4** | **+3** Chain Times | **-10%** Damage per Chain |
| **Level 5 (Alt)** | **Ricochet:** **+2** Chain Times. Projectiles can chain back to the **original target** if no other enemies are nearby. | **-20%** Less Damage (Flat).<br>Projectiles cannot pierce (chains consume the projectile). |

## Strategic Value
- **Pack Clearing:** Turns single-target projectiles into group-clearing tools.
- **Bossing (Lvl 5):** The Level 5 Ricochet variant allows projectiles to bounce between a boss and a wall (or minion) and hit the boss again, effectively doubling hits in specific scenarios.
