# Concentrated Area Support
![[Assets/Concentrated_Area_Rune.svg|128]]

## Description
Compresses the magic of the skill into a smaller point, drastically increasing its intensity and damage.

## Mechanics

### Progression
Each level grants more damage at the cost of area. Level 5 unlocks an **Alternate Behavior**.

| Level | Effect | Trade-off |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Level 1** | **+25%** More Area Damage | **-15%** Less Area of Effect |
| **Level 2** | **+35%** More Area Damage | **-20%** Less Area of Effect |
| **Level 3** | **+45%** More Area Damage | **-25%** Less Area of Effect |
| **Level 4** | **+55%** More Area Damage | **-30%** Less Area of Effect |
| **Level 5 (Alt)** | **Singularity:** **+100%** More Area Damage. Pulls nearby enemies towards the center on cast. | **-70%** Less Area of Effect (Pinpoint size). |

## Strategic Value
- **Boss Killer:** The single best rune for scaling AoE skills against single targets.
- **Pixel Perfect:** Requires precise aiming, especially with the Level 5 Singularity variant.
