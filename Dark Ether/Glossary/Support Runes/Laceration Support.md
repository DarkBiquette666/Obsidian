# Laceration Support
![[Assets/Laceration_Rune.svg|128]]

## Description
Enhances Physical skills to inflict deep, gapping wounds that refuse to close.

## Mechanics

### Progression
Level 5 unlocks an **Alternate Behavior**.

| Level | Effect | Trade-off |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Level 1** | **20%** Chance to inflict [[Open Wounds]] on Hit. | **-10%** Direct Damage |
| **Level 2** | **30%** Chance to inflict [[Open Wounds]] on Hit. | **-10%** Direct Damage |
| **Level 3** | **40%** Chance to inflict [[Open Wounds]] on Hit. | **-10%** Direct Damage |
| **Level 4** | **50%** Chance to inflict [[Open Wounds]] on Hit.<br>**Crits** always inflict Open Wounds. | **-10%** Direct Damage |
| **Level 5 (Alt)** | **Exsanguinate:** Hitting an enemy with **Open Wounds** causes them to spread a copy of the effect to nearby enemies (radius 3m). | **No Burst:** The "Hemorrhage" burst effect of Open Wounds is disabled.<br>Effect becomes pure spread/DoT focus. |

## Strategic Value
- **Anti-Tank:** Open Wounds ignores 50% physical resistance, making this rune the best choice against heavily armored foes.
- **Bleed Synergy:** Since Open Wounds is a separate ailment from Bleed, this allows a second layer of Physical DoT.
