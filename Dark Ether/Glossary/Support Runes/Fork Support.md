# Fork Support
![[Assets/Fork_Rune.svg|128]]

## Description
Projectiles split into two new projectiles upon impacting an enemy. These new projectiles travel at 60-degree angles from the original trajectory.

## Mechanics

### Progression
Each level improves the damage of the forked projectiles. Level 5 unlocks an **Alternate Behavior**.

| Level             | Effect                                                                                                      | Trade-off                                                  |
| :---------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Level 1**       | Projectiles **Fork** on Hit                                                                                 | **-30%** Less Damage for forked projectiles                |
| **Level 2**       | Projectiles **Fork** on Hit                                                                                 | **-20%** Less Damage for forked projectiles                |
| **Level 3**       | Projectiles **Fork** on Hit                                                                                 | **-10%** Less Damage for forked projectiles                |
| **Level 4**       | Projectiles **Fork** on Hit                                                                                 | **No Damage Penalty**                                      |
| **Level 5 (Alt)** | **Shatter:** Upon impact, projectile explodes into **4** smaller shards (Novas) instead of forking forward. | **-40%** Less Damage.<br>Shards have reduced range (-50%). |

## Strategic Value
- **Density Scaling:** The more enemies there are, the more projectiles are created. Exponentially powerful in dense crowds.
- **Shotgunning (Lvl 5):** The Shatter variant essentially turns any projectile into an impact grenade, great for overlapping damage on grouped enemies.
