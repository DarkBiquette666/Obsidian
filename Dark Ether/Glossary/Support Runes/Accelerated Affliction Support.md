# Accelerated Affliction Support
![[Assets/Accelerated_Affliction_Rune.svg|128]]

## Description
Accelerates the passage of time for the supported skill. For Damage over Time, this causes the damage to be dealt much faster. For delayed effects, they trigger sooner.

## Mechanics

### Progression
Level 5 unlocks an **Alternate Behavior**.

| Level | Effect | Trade-off |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Level 1** | **+10%** Faster Damage over Time | **-10%** Skill Duration |
| **Level 2** | **+15%** Faster Damage over Time | **-15%** Skill Duration |
| **Level 3** | **+20%** Faster Damage over Time | **-20%** Skill Duration |
| **Level 4** | **+25%** Faster Damage over Time | **-25%** Skill Duration |
| **Level 5 (Alt)** | **Collapse:** All Damage over Time is dealt **Instantly**. | **-50%** Less Total Damage.<br>Status effect is removed immediately. |

## Strategic Value
- **DPS Increase:** Drastically increases DPS (Damage Per Second) by compressing the total damage into a shorter window. Essential for fights where enemies die quickly.
- **Trap Activation:** Makes delayed skills trigger almost instantly.
- **Collapse (Lvl 5):** Turns DoT builds into Burst builds, allowing for hit-and-run tactics that deal immediate heavy damage.
