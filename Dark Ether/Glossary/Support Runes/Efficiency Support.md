# Efficiency Support
![[Assets/Efficiency_Rune.svg|128]]

## Description
A Support Rune that reduces the resource cost (Mana, Health, or Energy Shield) of the supported skill. Essential for sustaining high-cost abilities.

## Mechanics

### Progression
Each level improves cost reduction. Level 5 unlocks an **Alternate Behavior**.

| Level             | Effect                                             | Trade-off                                                                               |
| :---------------- | :------------------------------------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Level 1**       | **-20%** Reduced Resource Cost                     | **None**                                                                                |
| **Level 2**       | **-30%** Reduced Resource Cost                     | **None**                                                                                |
| **Level 3**       | **-40%** Reduced Resource Cost                     | **None**                                                                                |
| **Level 4**       | **-50%** Reduced Resource Cost                     | **None**                                                                                |
| **Level 5 (Alt)** | **Blood Magic:** Removes **Mana Cost** completely. | **Health Cost:** Skills cost **40%** of their original Mana cost as **Health** instead. |

## Compatible Skills
- **Blood Blade:** Reduces the substantial Health cost.
- **Vital Link:** Reduces mana drain.
- **Channeling Skills:** Crucial for maintaining high-cost spells.

## Strategic Value
- **Sustainability:** Allows high uptime on expensive skills.
- **Blood Magic (Lvl 5):** Enables "Mana-less" builds, freeing up Mana for Auras or allowing characters with low Mana pools to cast expensive spells.
