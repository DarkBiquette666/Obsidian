# Vitality Support
![[Assets/Vitality_Rune.svg|128]]

## Description
Grants the supported skill damage scaling based on the user's **Current Health**. This requires the user to maintain high health for maximum damage, creating a "Juggernaut" playstyle.

## Mechanics

### Progression
Each level increases the health-to-damage ratio. Level 5 unlocks an **Alternate Behavior**.

| Level | Effect | Trade-off |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Level 1** | Adds **1.0%** of **Current Health** as Flat Physical Damage | **10% Less** Attack & Cast Speed<br>User loses **1.0%** of Max Health per second. |
| **Level 2** | Adds **1.5%** of **Current Health** as Flat Physical Damage | **10% Less** Attack & Cast Speed<br>User loses **1.5%** of Max Health per second. |
| **Level 3** | Adds **2.0%** of **Current Health** as Flat Physical Damage | **10% Less** Attack & Cast Speed<br>User loses **2.0%** of Max Health per second. |
| **Level 4** | Adds **2.5%** of **Current Health** as Flat Physical Damage | **10% Less** Attack & Cast Speed<br>User loses **2.5%** of Max Health per second. |
| **Level 5 (Alt)** | **Retaliation:** Adds **4.0%** of **Missing Health** as Flat Physical Damage. | **20% Less** Attack & Cast Speed.<br>User loses **4.0%** of Max Health per second.<br>(Synergy: The health drain fuels the damage increase). |

## Compatible Skills
- **Blood Blade:** Thematic fit for Torin.
- **Melee Skills:** Allows tanks to deal damage.

## Strategic Value
- **Healthy Fighter:** Since damage scales on *Current* Health, getting hurt reduces your DPS. You must invest in Regeneration or Leech to stay at peak performance.
- **Retaliation (Lvl 5):** Flips the logic. The built-in Health Drain helps lower your HP to trigger the massive Missing Health bonus safely.
