# Homing Support
![[Assets/Homing_Rune.svg|128]]

## Description
Imbues projectiles with a magical consciousness, allowing them to adjust their trajectory to seek out targets.

## Mechanics

### Progression
Each level improves turn rate and target acquisition. Level 5 unlocks an **Alternate Behavior**.

| Level | Effect | Trade-off |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Level 1** | **Weak** Tracking (Slow turn rate) | **-25%** Projectile Speed |
| **Level 2** | **Moderate** Tracking | **-15%** Projectile Speed |
| **Level 3** | **Strong** Tracking (High turn rate) | **-10%** Projectile Speed |
| **Level 4** | **Perfect** Tracking (Cannot miss unless blocked) | **No Speed Penalty** |
| **Level 5 (Alt)** | **Predator:** Projectiles accelerate over time and have **Perfect** tracking. | **Limited Life:** Projectiles last 50% shorter duration.<br>They expire immediately if they miss their first pass (no circling back). |

## Strategic Value
- **Consistency:** Ensures damage connects, especially against mobile enemies.
- **Range Extension:** Slower projectiles (Levels 1-3) effectively travel further in terms of "time in air", often finding targets off-screen.
- **Predator (Lvl 5):** Excellent for long-range sniping where travel time is a factor.
