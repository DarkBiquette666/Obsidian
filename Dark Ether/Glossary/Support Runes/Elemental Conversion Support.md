# Elemental Conversion Support
![[Assets/Elemental_Conversion_Rune.svg|128]]

## Description
Converts Physical damage into Elemental damage (Fire, Cold, Lightning).

## Mechanics

### Configuration
When socketed, the player selects the target element (Fire, Cold, or Lightning).

### Progression
Each level improves conversion and elemental damage. Level 5 unlocks an **Alternate Behavior**.

| Level             | Effect                                                                                                                                         | Trade-off                                                                                            |
| :---------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Level 1**       | **50%** Physical converted to Element.<br>**+10%** Elemental Damage.                                                                           | **None**                                                                                             |
| **Level 2**       | **75%** Physical converted to Element.<br>**+15%** Elemental Damage.                                                                           | **None**                                                                                             |
| **Level 3**       | **100%** Physical converted to Element.<br>**+20%** Elemental Damage.                                                                          | **None**                                                                                             |
| **Level 4**       | **100%** Physical converted to Element.<br>**+25%** Elemental Damage.<br>Damage penetrates **10%** Resistance.                                 | **None**                                                                                             |
| **Level 5 (Alt)** | **Ether Conversion:** **50%** Physical converted to **Random Element** each hit.<br>Gain **30%** of Physical Damage as **Extra Ether Damage**. | **Unpredictable:** Cannot target specific elemental weaknesses easily.<br>No Resistance Penetration. |

## Compatible Skills
- **Blood Blade:** Elemental bleed builds.
- **Physical Spells:** Thorn Spike -> Fire Spike.

## Strategic Value
- **Resistance Penetration:** Bypasses Physical Resistance.
- **Chaos (Lvl 5):** Adds a powerful "Extra Damage" layer which is rare and potent, at the cost of consistency.
