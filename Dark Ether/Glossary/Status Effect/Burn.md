**Burn** is a status effect that deals fire damage over time. The base damage is proportional to the triggering hit. By default, only one stack is allowed. Each new application refreshes the duration of the current stack and updates the damage if the new damage exceeds the current stack damage.

Each new application increases the intensity of the stack proportionally to the strength of the hit that applies it and the total target Health. The final damage of the stack is increased by the intensity. Once the intensity reaches 100%, the stack is consumed, causing an explosion in an area of effect (AoE) around the target and dealing damage proportional to the stack's accumulated damage.

Intensity decreased overtime (1% per second)
